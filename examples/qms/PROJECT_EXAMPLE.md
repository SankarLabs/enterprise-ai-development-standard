# Example: QMS project discovery

Illustrative only; not an approved solution or assumed requirement.

A QMS discovery should explore:
- Document control, versioning and controlled distribution.
- Nonconformance, CAPA, deviations and change control.
- Training and competency records.
- Audit planning, findings, evidence and follow-up.
- Approval workflows, electronic signatures and identity assurance.
- Traceability, immutable audit history and retention.
- Applicable regulatory/quality obligations and accountable compliance reviewers.
- Separation of duties and role-based access.
- Validation strategy, test evidence and change impact.
- Integration with ERP, manufacturing, identity and document repositories.

Do not claim regulatory compliance from templates alone. Engage the organization's quality, legal and compliance owners.
