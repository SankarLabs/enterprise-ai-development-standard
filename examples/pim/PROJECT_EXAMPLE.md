# Example: PIM project discovery

Illustrative only; not an approved solution or assumed requirement.

A PIM discovery should explore:
- Product domains, attributes, variants, categories and relationships.
- Product content ownership, stewardship and approval workflows.
- Source-of-truth systems and downstream consumers.
- Supplier onboarding and product data ingestion.
- Digital assets, localization and channel-specific enrichment.
- APIs, events, batch feeds and ERP/commerce integrations.
- Data quality rules, completeness, validation and duplicate handling.
- Role-based access, audit trails and change history.
- Search, performance, scale, availability and operational ownership.
- Migration, reconciliation and coexistence with existing catalog systems.

Do not select a database, event broker or microservice architecture until the requirements, constraints and trade-offs have been reviewed.
