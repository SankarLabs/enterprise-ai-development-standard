# Example: LMS project discovery

Illustrative only; not an approved solution or assumed requirement.

An LMS discovery should explore:
- Learners, instructors, administrators and organizational hierarchy.
- Course catalog, learning paths, cohorts and enrollment.
- Content formats, assessments, completion and certification.
- Progress tracking, prerequisites, reminders and notifications.
- Accessibility, localization and mobile experience.
- Identity, SSO, HRIS and content-provider integrations.
- Reporting, analytics, data retention and privacy.
- Peak usage, video/content delivery, availability and support.
- Roles, delegated administration and audit needs.

Do not assume one learning standard, hosting model or content platform without validating the business and integration requirements.
